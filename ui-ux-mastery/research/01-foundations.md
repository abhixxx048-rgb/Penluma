# Research Notes — Chapter 01: Foundations

## Definitions (Nielsen Norman Group / Don Norman & Jakob Nielsen)
- NN/G: "User experience encompasses all aspects of the end-user's interaction with the company, its services, and its products."
- UX goes far beyond "giving customers what they say they want, or providing checklist features."
- UI is just ONE component of the broader experience (movie review site example: perfect UI but missing indie films = bad UX).
- Usability = "a quality attribute of the UI, covering whether the system is easy to learn, efficient to use, pleasant." Total UX is broader than usability.

## Origin of the term "user experience"
- Don Norman coined it, joined Apple 1993 as "User Experience Architect" — first use of the phrase in a job title.
- Norman: "I invented the term because I thought human interface and usability were too narrow. I wanted to cover all aspects of the person's experience with the system including industrial design graphics, the interface, the physical interaction and the manual."
- Formed the "User Experience Architect's Office" with Tom Erickson and Harry Saddler.
- Brenda Laurel used "user experience" earlier in "Interface as Mimesis" (1986).
- Norman known as "father of user experience"; co-founded Nielsen Norman Group with Jakob Nielsen.

## Jesse James Garrett — The Elements of User Experience (five planes)
- Planes from abstract (bottom) to concrete (top): Strategy, Scope, Structure, Skeleton, Surface.
- Each depends on the one below; ripple effect upward.
- Strategy: product objectives (business) + user needs.
- Scope: features and content — what the product will do.
- Structure: information architecture + interaction design — how it's organized, how it behaves.
- Skeleton: information design, navigation design, interface design — placement of elements.
- Surface: sensory/visual design — what users see first.

## Dieter Rams — Ten Principles of Good Design
1. Innovative 2. Useful 3. Aesthetic 4. Makes a product understandable 5. Honest
6. Unobtrusive 7. Long-lasting 8. Thorough down to the last detail (consistent)
9. Environmentally friendly 10. As little design as possible.
- Braun / Vitsoe designer; Jony Ive credited Rams for Apple's aesthetic.

## Design thinking (Stanford d.school / IDEO)
- Five modes: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test.
- Not strictly sequential — iterative, parallel modes.

## UX disciplines
- User research: needs & problems via interviews, surveys, field studies.
- Information architecture: organizing, labeling, structuring content for findability.
- Interaction design: how systems respond to input; states, feedback, flows.
- Visual/UI design: look and feel, hierarchy, brand.
- Content design / UX writing: clear, concise language.

## Industrial design roots
- Raymond Loewy — "father of industrial design"; MAYA principle (Most Advanced Yet Acceptable).
- Dieter Rams — good design as manifesto.
