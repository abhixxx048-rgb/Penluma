### 📌 Posting tips
- One topic per tweet. Lead with the hook line, drop the link last.
- Turn any into a thread: post the hook, then pull 3–4 sub-articles from that topic folder as replies.
- Pin the "Why Writing Makes You Think Clearly" essay — it explains the whole brand.
- 28 topics = ~4 weeks of daily tweets if you post one per day.
